1. Purpose of the Website

This website is provided for informational and venue inquiry purposes regarding the charitable activities, cultural programs, leadership, and banquet venue facilities of Shree Bhatiawadi Trust, Mumbai.

Inquiries submitted through this website do not constitute a confirmed venue booking. All banquet and hall bookings are subject to formal verification, availability confirmation, date allocation, and official reservation agreements executed directly with the Trust office.

5. Inquiries & Assistance

For any questions regarding trust regulations or hall booking terms, please contact the Trust administrative office at 022-21028801 or visit us at Bhatia Wadi, Mumbai.
