Privacy Policy

Official Trust Policy • Shree Bhatiawadi Trust, Mumbai

Shree Bhatiawadi Trust ("the Trust", "we", "our") is dedicated to charitable service, cultural preservation, and community welfare. We hold the privacy of our patrons, community members, and website visitors in the highest regard.

01 Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to provide when inquiring about banquet hall bookings, cultural events, charitable initiatives, or general trust services through our contact form, email, or telephone. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, and event details.

02 How We Use Your Information

Any information shared with us is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your booking inquiries, questions, and community service requests.
  • Coordinate venue availability and ceremonial arrangements.
  • Fulfill legal and charitable record-keeping requirements as mandated by regulatory authorities.

03 Confidentiality & Protection

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to any third parties or marketing agencies. Information is maintained with appropriate confidentiality and access is restricted to authorized Trust personnel solely for administrative coordination.

04 Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to update any contact details submitted to the Trust, please contact us directly or visit our office.
