OUR STORY

The Story Behind Our Service

The story of Shree Bhatiawadi Trust begins in the heart of Mumbai, where a group of dedicated community leaders came together with a shared vision of creating lasting positive change.

Through decades of dedicated service, we have grown from a small community initiative into a respected institution that touches thousands of lives each year.

Our journey has been marked by continuous evolution while staying true to our core values of compassion, integrity, and cultural preservation.

OUR JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

Milestones That Shaped Our Story

Milestones that shaped our organization and strengthened our commitment to the community

1950

Foundation

Shree Bhatiawadi Trust was established by visionary community leaders with a mission to serve and uplift the community through charitable works.

1965

First Banquet Hall

Construction of our first banquet hall was completed, providing a beautiful venue for community celebrations and gatherings.

1980

Expansion

Major expansion project undertaken to add more facilities and enhance our charitable programs across the region.

1995

Modernization

Complete renovation and modernization of facilities while preserving the traditional architectural elements and cultural heritage.

2010

New Era

Introduction of state-of-the-art amenities and technology, making our venues among the finest in the area.

2020

Community Impact

Reached milestone of serving over 100,000 community members through various charitable initiatives and programs.
