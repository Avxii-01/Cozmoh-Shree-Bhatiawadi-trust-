❖ OUR SACRED MISSION ❖

Serving Communities. Preserving Heritage.

◇

Rooted in generations of devotion, Shree Bhatiawadi Trust is dedicated to uplifting society through selfless charitable service, preserving rich cultural heritage, and nurturing vibrant community spaces where traditions flourish and sacred bonds are strengthened.

LEARN ABOUT US →
01

Charitable Service

Supporting community welfare through various charitable initiatives, educational support, and healthcare programs designed to uplift the community.

02

Cultural Heritage

Preserving and promoting traditional Bhatia and Kutchi values, arts, and cultural practices to ensure our rich legacy is passed down to future generations.

03

Community Space

Providing elegant, well-maintained venues for celebrations, spiritual gatherings, and community events that foster unity and joy.
