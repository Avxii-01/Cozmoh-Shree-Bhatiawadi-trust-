OUR TRUSTEES

The People Behind Our Purpose.

Meet the dedicated leaders who guide our mission and uphold our values with wisdom and integrity.

LEADERSHIP & GOVERNANCE

Guided by Experience.
United by Service.

Our board of trustees brings together diverse expertise and unwavering commitment to serve the community. Each member contributes their unique skills and experience to ensure the trust continues to thrive and fulfill its charitable mission.

Dr. Eleanor Vance

CHAIRPERSON

Dr. Eleanor Vance brings over 25 years of experience in non-profit leadership and strategic planning. Her vision has been instrumental in guiding the organization's growth and impact. She holds a PhD in Social Sciences from the University of Cambridge.

APPOINTED March 2008
SERVICE 16 Years

Mr. David Chen

SECRETARY

Mr. David Chen is a seasoned legal professional with a focus on corporate governance and compliance. His meticulous attention to detail ensures the smooth operation of board affairs and adherence to all regulatory standards. He joined the board in 2015.

APPOINTED July 2015
SERVICE 9 Years

Ms. Sarah Jenkins

TREASURER

Ms. Sarah Jenkins is a Chartered Accountant with extensive experience in financial management and auditing for charitable organizations. She oversees the financial health and sustainability of the trust, ensuring transparency and accountability.

APPOINTED January 2020
SERVICE 4 Years

Prof. Robert Maxwell

TRUSTEE MEMBER

Professor Robert Maxwell is a distinguished academic in environmental studies, bringing valuable insights into sustainable practices and community engagement. His expertise helps shape the organization's long-term environmental initiatives.

APPOINTED September 2017
SERVICE 7 Years

Ms. Anya Sharma

TRUSTEE MEMBER

Ms. Anya Sharma is a passionate advocate for youth development and education. With a background in public policy, she contributes to programs aimed at empowering the next generation and fostering inclusive communities.

SERVICE 2 Years

Mr. Michael O'Connell

TRUSTEE MEMBER
